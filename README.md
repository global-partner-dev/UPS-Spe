Descrição:

We are seeking an experienced web developer to create a professional website for our company, which specializes in Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) technology. The primary goal of the website is to effectively showcase our company's services and portfolio to potential clients. We require a site that is visually appealing, user-friendly, and responsive across all devices.

The project should be delivered quickly and at a reasonable price, without compromising on quality. It is essential that the website does not include any integrated payment system, as its function is purely informational and promotional.

We are open to different technological approaches, including development using WordPress for ease of content management, or a custom coding stack for greater flexibility and performance. The chosen freelancer must be able to advise on the best approach for our specific needs.

Upon completion, a guaranteed post-delivery maintenance period is required to ensure the website remains functional and up-to-date. We are looking for a freelancer who can provide a comprehensive solution from development to initial support.

Hello David,

For a UPS company, the website has to earn trust quickly, so the goal is to make your services and past work look credible and make it easy for a client to ask for a quote.

I would build it in WordPress, because you will want to update services and portfolio entries yourself without calling a developer.
I would use a lightweight custom theme instead of a heavy page-builder template, which keeps the site fast and the code clean.

The portfolio would be a custom post type, so each project has its own page with photos, the type of installation, and the result.
Your team can add new projects from the dashboard in a few minutes.

Since there is no payment system, the main conversion point is a contact and quote request form.
I would add spam protection and email delivery through SMTP, because forms on new sites often fail silently and you lose leads without knowing.

The layout would be designed mobile-first, since many technical buyers check suppliers from their phones.
I would also handle image optimization, basic SEO (titles, descriptions, sitemap) and caching, so the site loads well and can be found for searches like "UPS supplier" in your region.

The most common cause of delays in projects like this is waiting for texts, logos and photos.
To avoid that, I would send you a short content checklist on day one and start building with placeholders while you prepare the material.

After delivery, I will provide a support period for fixes, adjustments and plugin or security updates.
I would also give you a short walkthrough so you can manage the content on your own.

Two quick questions before I start:
Do you already have a logo, brand colors and photos, or should the design start from scratch?
Will the site be in one language or more than one?

I am ready to start as soon as we align on these points.
